# University Attendance Regulations and Policy Framework

## Section 1: Introduction and Scope
The University expects all enrolled students to attend every scheduled learning and teaching session associated with their registered courses. Regular attendance and active participation are fundamental components of academic success, intellectual growth, and professional development. This comprehensive policy framework applies to all undergraduate and postgraduate students enrolled in taught programs across all faculties and departments. It establishes the minimum statutory requirements for attendance, the mechanisms for monitoring student presence, and the administrative procedures for enforcing these requirements. The University reserves the right to amend these regulations in response to educational directives or statutory requirements, ensuring that all modifications are communicated to the student body in a timely and transparent manner.

## Section 2: Definitions
For the purposes of this regulatory framework, the following definitions shall apply:
- **Scheduled Session:** Any lecture, seminar, tutorial, laboratory practical, workshop, field trip, or assessment explicitly listed in the course timetable.
- **Authorized Absence:** An absence that has been formally approved by the relevant academic authority prior to the event, or retroactively in cases of documented emergencies.
- **Unauthorized Absence:** Any absence from a scheduled session without prior approval or valid retroactive justification.
- **Academic Authority:** The designated faculty member, department chair, or university official responsible for overseeing academic conduct and attendance within a specific program.

## Section 3: General Attendance Requirements
The academic integrity of the University's programs relies heavily on student engagement. Therefore, all students must maintain a high level of attendance. Specifically, students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each course to be eligible to sit for the final examination. This threshold is absolute and reflects the minimum acceptable level of engagement required to achieve the learning outcomes of the course. The University recognizes that unforeseen circumstances may occasionally prevent attendance. However, no exceptions will be made for attendance falling below 65% under any circumstances. Any student whose attendance falls below this 65% absolute baseline will be automatically deregistered from the course and awarded a failing grade, regardless of any medical, personal, or extenuating factors.

## Section 4: Recording and Monitoring of Attendance
The University employs a multifaceted approach to monitor student attendance. Course instructors are primarily responsible for accurately recording attendance during each scheduled session. This may be achieved through manual roll calls, biometric scanning, or electronic ID card swiping systems. The collected data is centralized in the University's Student Information System (SIS), allowing academic advisors and administrative staff to track student engagement in real-time. Students are strongly encouraged to periodically review their attendance records on the SIS portal to ensure accuracy and address any discrepancies promptly.

## Section 5: Leave of Absence
Students anticipating a prolonged absence due to significant personal, medical, or professional reasons must formally apply for a Leave of Absence. Such applications must be submitted to the Academic Registry at least two weeks prior to the intended start date of the leave. A Leave of Absence may be granted for a maximum of one academic year. During this period, the student's academic status is preserved, but they are not permitted to attend classes, utilize university facilities, or participate in assessments. Reinstatement following a Leave of Absence requires a formal petition and approval from the Dean of Students.

## Section 6: Consequences of Low Attendance
Failure to meet the minimum attendance requirements without authorized absence will result in progressive disciplinary and academic actions. Initially, students falling below the 85% mark will receive a formal warning via their official university email. Continued failure to attend may lead to an academic probationary period, during which the student must meet with their academic advisor to develop an attendance improvement plan. As stated in Section 3, falling below the mandatory thresholds will ultimately lead to exclusion from final examinations and potential academic dismissal.

## Section 7: Re-admission and Appeals
Students who have been academically dismissed due to severe attendance violations may apply for re-admission after a mandatory cooling-off period of one academic semester. The re-admission process involves a comprehensive review of the student's circumstances and a demonstrated commitment to adhering to university regulations moving forward. Students have the right to appeal any disciplinary action related to attendance. Appeals must be lodged with the University Appeals Committee within ten working days of the disciplinary decision, detailing the grounds for the appeal and providing any relevant supporting documentation.

## Section 8: Role of Course Instructors
Course instructors play a pivotal role in implementing the attendance policy. Beyond merely recording presence, instructors are expected to identify patterns of absenteeism early and intervene appropriately. This may involve reaching out to the student directly, referring them to academic support services, or notifying the relevant department chair. Instructors must also ensure that the attendance requirements specific to their course are clearly articulated in the course syllabus and discussed during the first week of classes.

## Section 9: International Students and Visa Compliance
International students studying on a student visa are subject to additional statutory attendance requirements mandated by national immigration authorities. The University is legally obligated to report significant unauthorized absences to the relevant immigration office. Failure to maintain satisfactory attendance may result in the revocation of the student visa, leading to immediate deportation and a ban on re-entry. International students must familiarize themselves with these specific requirements and consult with the International Student Office regarding any attendance concerns.

## Section 10: Amendments and Review
This policy framework is subject to biennial review by the University Senate to ensure its continued relevance and effectiveness. Any proposed amendments must be debated and approved by the Senate before implementation. Students and faculty will be notified of any changes via the official university bulletin at least one semester prior to the changes taking effect.
